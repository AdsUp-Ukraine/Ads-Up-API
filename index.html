Ads_Up API

Internal tool used by Andrii Tykhodiuk to manage and report on Google Ads accounts via the Google Ads API.

Privacy Policy

This application is used solely by its owner, Andrii Tykhodiuk, to access Google Ads accounts he manages via the Google Ads API.

  • Data accessed: Google Ads account and performance data, used only for campaign reporting and optimization.
  • Data is not sold, shared with, or transferred to third parties.
  • Data is stored only on the owner's devices and is not used for any purpose other than managing the owner's Google Ads accounts.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
